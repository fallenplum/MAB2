This rules out treating poor prediction as the only bottleneck, and freezes the current annotation foundation as a failed candidate. It does not end AutoResearch. A later mechanism requires genuinely useful decision information, full-cost superiority to free filtering/structural rules and independent/vote controls, and fresh question groups. No new formal candidate is automatically admitted by this report.
