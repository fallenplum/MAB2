# Mechanism study draft

`main.tex` is the ACL-style source and `main.pdf` is the locally compiled author-review draft. The PDF is three pages and was compiled with the ACL style included in this directory. Recompile with `pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex` twice in a directory containing `acl.sty`.

The manuscript reports evidence from M50, M54, M56, M58, and M59. The primary claims are restricted to the finite four-row interface and declared token accounting. M50 hardware qualification and the M56 active-allocation gate are negative. M59 establishes physical decision-equivalence for its three modes, while its current cost aggregation is descriptive and does not support a deployment-speed claim. Role permutations share questions and are not independent datasets.

Question-level arrays, answer keys, model weights, credentials, and server paths remain private. The public scripts reconstruct the frozen manifests and test the finite solver without exposing those artifacts.
