# Decision-value foundation

[Primary paper](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/070693424), [author-hosted PDF](https://optimallearning.princeton.edu/Papers/FrazierPowellDayanik_KnowledgeGradientSICON.pdf).

Single-step expected improvement in the best posterior decision is established knowledge-gradient methodology. The cited paper assumes independent normal populations with known common variance. Its guarantees are not transferred to our ternary paired outcomes or graph-dependent costs. Consequently, the EVSI criterion is not an innovation claim.

This is a supplementary foundation record, not an additional reproduced graph-agent baseline. Read scope is recorded in RECORD.json. Howard (1966) was also searched, but direct IEEE access returned an anti-bot page; it was not added as a primary-source-verified content citation. No experiment protocol, nominee, threshold or outcome was changed.
