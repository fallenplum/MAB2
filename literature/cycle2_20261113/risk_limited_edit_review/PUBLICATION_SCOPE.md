# Publication scope

This is a bounded targeted novelty review, not an exhaustive systematic review. The paper registry records reading depth and publication/version limits. The raw search responses and arXiv metadata/abstract XML are retained privately rather than republished; their original hashes remain in the review receipt. The public search file is an explicitly labelled excerpt of query strings and source URLs.

Root spot-checked the official PMLR entries for aLTT (ICML 2025) and Off-Policy Confidence Sequences (ICML 2021), and the official NeurIPS 2020 entry for Sequential Bayesian Experimental Design with Variable Cost Structure. Their metadata and stated problem scopes support the review's conservative novelty assessment. This check does not re-prove their theorems or validate a new graph-edit policy.
