Together with AgentConductor, LEMON, CANTANTE and C3, these sources reject claims that changing code-agent graphs, counterfactual local credit, caching, visible-test selection, adaptive budget allocation or stale-predictor monitoring alone are novel. Candidate distinction remains a measured graph-edit-specific credit-maintenance/feedback-acquisition mechanism with equal predictors, legal information, candidate actions and full cost. No distinct effective mechanism has yet been established.

Three reviewer perspectives: method attribution requires separating generic repair from the incremental graph-change signal; evaluation requires preserving old generated programs when their history is available; deployment requires counting training/generation/feedback and avoiding oracle reward access. Advantages of the current screen are frozen strong simple controls and hidden-label isolation. Limitations are absent published-method reproduction, a small controlled APPS-training subset, limited case coverage and unverified pretraining overlap. Existing gates remain unchanged; this update does not re-label a failed route as successful.
