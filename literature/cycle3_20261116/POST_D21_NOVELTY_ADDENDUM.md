# Long-document applicability is a substrate change, not a novelty claim

| Existing evidence line | Established capability | Formal9 distinction | Required future evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qasper, NAACL2021, verified official evaluator | Paper-grounded QA and answer/evidence evaluation | Original TRAIN paper-level grouping and longer complete text;16-paper screen only | Fresh64-paper confirmation plus separately reserved transfer after policy freeze |
| LongBench v2, ACL2025 | Long-context multi-task evaluation |44 feasible single-document context groups reserved; D20 two-domain NO-GO retained | Transfer cannot claim entire benchmark or longest contexts |
| C3 v4, trace-state counterfactual credit (POST_D18 records) | Matched-prefix counterfactual continuations and credit | Exact-ID communication constrains evidence loss; not an independent counterfactual-credit invention | Budgeted maintenance must beat matched feedback baselines |
| CAGE-Cal, topology calibration (POST_D18 records) | Calibration and selection over communication structures | All speculative branches/feedback must be charged | Equal complete acquisition and service cost; no free candidate panels |
| EIB-Learner/evidence compression and original Qasper retrieval controls | Learning/selecting evidence for QA | Literal256-token substrings and full-context/flat/BM25/dense/three-vote controls | Extraction alone, a new dataset or a different receiver is insufficient novelty |

Formal9 asks whether a usable quality/credit regime exists at all. It is not a fixed-predictor G3 revalidation comparison. A pass would permit29-paper development, followed by a frozen policy evaluated on64 fresh papers. A failure ends this applicability route while preserving all former negative results. No stronger claim follows from hardware qualification, oracle headroom or official-metric parity.

Potential advantage: true long-document information distribution gives a concrete reason communication might matter. Main risks: simple retrieval dominates, source selection destroys evidence, or conditional credit remains stochastic. F1 is chosen before generation for this new dataset using its official evaluator; this does not reinterpret prior EM failures.
