# MAB2 autonomous research acceptance contract, 2026-11-13

## Authority and scope

The user authorizes reversible research on `research/autonomous-20261113` from
commit `171bb65953e0bb0a14a6fb5e3c4118fb55fde112`, using existing hardware.
New compute is capped at160 aggregate GPU occupancy hours and5 formal candidate
iterations. Failures, qualification and repeats count. Historical costs remain
separate. Two CPU diagnostic rounds (each≤4 process-hours) precede new inference.
No paid compute, privilege expansion, deletion or irreversible action is covered.

## Scientific controls

The scientific question is whether graph-conditioned credit maintenance and
feedback acquisition improve future collaborative inference at equal complete
cost. G2's negative predictive gate and D1's unestablished decision gains remain
valid historical results. The new route is separately authorized and versioned.
Old G2 train/tune/test are development data now; official final test stays sealed.

Every round records hypothesis, falsifier, fixed metric, fair baseline, exact
operator, permitted information, sampling/seed design, code/input hashes,
implementation checks, complete outcomes and a keep/reject decision. Protocol
commits precede results; post-hoc work never becomes independent confirmation.
Each method receives only its own purchased or authorized feedback. Shared
physical cache savings never create free policy observations. Question blocks
preserve correlations; a graph's quality label is not duplicated as independent
evidence. Current-question plans precede generation; feedback affects future
questions only. Paused edges share the same exploration floor.

## Decision and stopping rules

All formal confirmations specify an effect threshold, independent question/stream
count, fixed seeds, family of comparisons and multiplicity before generation.
If a predictor lacks useful decision signal, its architecture is not expanded
without a diagnosed failure mechanism. A feedback-only conservative candidate
may be tested under a fresh contract, with identical editor/budget for baselines.
No new formal run starts with unresolved leakage, integrity, reservation, cost,
observation-permission or baseline-fidelity defects. A failed confirmation is
retained and cannot be rescued by tuning its test set. At the budget/iteration
cap, stop, archive and give the remaining claims their actual status.

## Manuscript gate

A full ACL-style manuscript requires: supported central claim on independent
data; same-complete-budget random/periodic/age/uncertainty/graph-ablation controls;
at least2 tasks and5 algorithm/stream seeds as retained from the original minimum
contract; at least2 audited close baselines; reproduction of the effective
method; ablations, recovery tests, complete cost ledger, independent statistical
replay, verified bibliography and independent reviewer checks. If these cannot
fit this research cycle, deliver the audit and limited evidence package with
paper status INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; do not lower the gate to manufacture a paper.

## Skill policy

research-rigor is the controller; autoresearch manages bounded iterations.
paper-lookup/literature-review support primary-source work; scientific-writing
and peer-review apply when their artifacts are justified. No automatic external
review upload, paid image/API service, messaging, or perpetual automation.
Skill/library outputs cannot substitute for original evidence or human approval.
