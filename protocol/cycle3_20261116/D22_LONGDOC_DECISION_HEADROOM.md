# D22A: optimistic long-document decision ceiling with sunk prefix costs

Frozen after Formal9 exposed pilot results and before this diagnostic. Formal9 failed three gates and remains STOP_FORMAL9_APPLICABILITY_ROUTE. D22 is exploratory, not a modified pass gate or independent confirmation. Its question is narrower: does even a perfect current-prefix legal-deletion selector have enough complete service value to warrant a new mechanism study?

Use only the16 exposed pilot papers, both original seeds and archived complete G1/G1_MINUS_EU/G1_MINUS_EV terminals. Controller observes the CURRENT G1 source/relay outputs U,V,R, so EVERY legal action pays ALL those prefix input+output tokens once, including a source that becomes dead after deletion. Do not use the smaller nominal deleted-graph prefix cost as a free observation. Add the selected terminal's input+output cost. No feedback acquisition cost is charged to this oracle: explicitly optimistic necessary bound, never an attainable method score. A real policy must later pay every queried/discarded terminal and all feedback.

Oracle maximizes F1-5e-6*tokens among keep/delete-U-M/delete-V-M, ties prefer keep then U then V. Compute fixed-action scores under the identical observed-prefix accounting and compare to retrospective best fixed action (optimistic baseline). Strong direct controls retain their actual full costs: FLAT,DENSE,BM25,SINGLE_Q,SINGLE_M,VOTE_Q3. Controller could choose to run a direct control instead, but that is routing and is not evidence for graph-credit maintenance; exclude it from the legal oracle actions.

Necessary-screen PASS requires oracle mean utility at least.01 above EACH strong direct control and best fixed legal action; positive utility gain over DENSE,FLAT and best fixed legal action in EACH source seed; oracle raw F1 no worse than DENSE/FLAT by>.02; at least4 distinct papers have a positive oracle utility gain over current keep in BOTH seeds. These are new diagnostic thresholds, not edits of Formal9. A FAIL freezes this within-question direct-edge revalidation route on the current operator; no additional crossed decoding on this sample. A PASS only permits designing a new diagnostic before new GPU work. No policy is selected/trained, no test labels, new model calls, CI or bootstrap; CPU<=1hour,0GPU.

Advantage: a sunk-cost optimistic ceiling can rule out an expensive approach before new inference. Risks: oracle labels are unavailable at decision time, n16 is small, and a positive ceiling may be entirely unattainable after acquisition charges. A positive answer is headroom only. Any eventual budgeted method must beat random,periodic,uncertainty,graph-change and cheap static/certificate controls under identical full budgets and fixed initialization.
