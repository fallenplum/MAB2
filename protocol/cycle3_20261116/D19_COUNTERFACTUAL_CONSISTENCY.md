# D19: gold-free counterfactual consistency foundation

Exploratory zero-new-inference diagnostic after D18 fixed cross-question message predictors failed. Freeze those failures; do not tune their features or models. New hypothesis: direct comparisons of already executed legal graph continuations provide more useful per-query information than predicting credit across questions. This is a strong baseline/foundation screen, NOT a novelty claim for majority vote, early stopping, counterfactual continuation, or topology ensembles.

Use all D16 outcomes, same96 question groups per task,2source realizations,4 terminal RNG anchors. Each anchor is a separate service simulation, not a free vote. Administrative shock and legal actions as D17. All three source messages are generated before the shock and paid once in message-observing methods. No gold, scored EM, known correctness, predictor fitting or terminal-output feature is available before querying that terminal continuation. Gold is read only by the separate scorer AFTER the complete policy trace is frozen.

Nominee ADAPT_GRAPH: query current graph B at anchor RNG. Order the two legal single-edge deletions by deterministic terminal INPUT token count ascending, tie by mask. Query the first not-identical request; stop when two DISTINCT request identities yield the same normalized answer. Otherwise query the second distinct deletion; return plurality among acquired distinct requests, tie by original current-graph answer then acquisition order. Identical full generation requests are cache hits, pay once and never count as an additional vote. Empty normalized answers cannot form a consensus. No semantic matching or label-informed alias matching; use the existing official answer normalizer.

Baselines: KEEP_CURRENT (one call, all pre-shock sources paid), SAME_GRAPH_SC (current graph at anchor,(anchor+1)%4,(anchor+2)%4, same early stop), NO_NOTE_SC (graph0, same3 seeds/early stop, no source cost), NO_NOTE_SINGLE, training-fold selected fixed policy from D18 (nominal selected-source cost only), RANDOM_ORDER_GRAPH (same legal graphs with first deletion determined by a fixed SHA256 of task/index/source/anchor), and ALL_GRAPH_VOTE (all three legal graphs, no early stop). All queries use actual archived generations; charge each distinct executed request's input+output plus required prefixes, including discarded probes. Repeats across service simulations are charged again; no cross-replication free cache. Report number of unique requests and fully charged token utility EM-5e-6*tokens. Baseline early stopping uses the exact same consensus and tie rules.

Frozen necessary screen: nominee mean utility>=each of NO_NOTE_SINGLE, NO_NOTE_SC, SAME_GRAPH_SC and D18 fixed by+.005 on EACH task; gains positive in both source realizations and>=3 of4 terminal anchors for each comparison. Report random-order and full-graph comparisons but do not call an ordering-only saving a research contribution. Correct implementation should make early-stopped and full three-graph voting answers identical; verify this for all cells (duplicates excluded, original-first tie priority), while allowing costs to differ. A failure freezes this gold-free local-consistency foundation. A pass only permits a new acquisition mechanism and independent full-cost confirmation. No new GPU hours, formal round, CI or bootstrap.

Closest prior boundaries: CAGE-Cal/Select(arXiv2605.30653v1) already calibrates matched no-communication versus communication states and runs candidate topologies before selecting; C3(arXiv2603.06859v4) already executes trace-conditioned counterfactual continuations for reward credit. Neither generic idea is new here. A future claim would require a demonstrated graph-change-specific acquisition benefit under a complete budget. Advantages: no service gold and no learned generalization assumption; risk: correlated wrong answers, additional calls may erase gains, small exposed star and simple vote normalization.
