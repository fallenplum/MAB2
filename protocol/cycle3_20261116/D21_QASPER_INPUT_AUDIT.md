# D21: separate-corpus long-document feasibility, input only

D20 is NO-GO for its proposed two-domain LongBench-v2 pilot:44 single-document groups and23 multi-document groups, below the frozen24-per-domain minimum. Do not lower that threshold or split a shared-context group to recover the missing unit. This new scope instead develops on the original Qasper TRAIN corpus and reserves LongBench-v2 single-document data for later cross-dataset transfer. It changes the sampling frame, not D20's verdict. MuSiQue/LongBench-v1 were considered from documentation only; their examples were not downloaded/read and their test data will not be used for development.
