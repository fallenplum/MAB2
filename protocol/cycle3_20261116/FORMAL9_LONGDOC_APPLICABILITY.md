## Hypothesis and falsifiers
H9: when evidence is genuinely dispersed across long papers, exchanging exact original passages can maintain answer quality while a graph change alters the usefulness of a direct communication edge in a reproducible way. This is a necessary applicability test for later budgeted revalidation; extraction, graph counterfactuals and prefix-state credit themselves are established tools, not novelty. Advantages: preserves literal evidence and gives full-context controls all information. Risks: selectors can lose evidence; retrieval/flat extraction may dominate; only16 pilot papers give limited screening precision; stochastic credit can remain unstable. A positive pilot only unlocks development and independent confirmation.
