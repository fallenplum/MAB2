## Hierarchy and continuation

1. Candidate Failure eliminates only the named implementation/hypothesis under
   its frozen comparison. Record what failed and continue with another distinct
   candidate. No user permission is needed for an in-scope replacement.
2. Research Direction Failure freezes the narrow mechanism family. The outer loop
   returns to primary literature and failure mechanisms, then derives and tests
   alternatives within the graph-credit/feedback research question.
3. Project Stop requires an evidenced project-level reason: exhausted aggregate
   160 GPU occupancy hours or 5 formal candidate rounds; no authorized feasible
   work after documented environment repair attempts and local fallback; an
   essential permission outside the authorization boundary; user cancellation;
   or exhaustion of materially distinct in-scope questions after documented
   outer-loop analysis. One null, an unfinished novelty review, or an audit
   report is never a project completion/stop condition. An inconclusive pilot
   does not prove a direction impossible. Scope exhaustion must enumerate
   considered alternatives and why no remaining valid experiment fits scope.
4. Supported final findings permit finalization only after the requested evidence,
   statistical checks, reproduction, artifact delivery and manuscript audit.

rounds; prior autonomous usage is 0 GPU hours and 0 formal rounds. A formal round
begins with the first nonqualification model generation for a registered
candidate; its planned baselines, ablations, repeats and confirmation count as
that candidate, while a substantive new algorithm/dataset-driven hypothesis is
another candidate. All hardware occupancy and failures count either way.
